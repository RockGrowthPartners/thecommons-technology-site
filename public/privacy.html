Privacy

Last updated November 2, 2026

This page explains how The Commons Technology, Inc. (“we”) handles information on this website and in our apps.

This website

We don’t use cookies, analytics or advertising trackers on thecommons.technology. Our hosting provider processes standard request data, such as IP address and browser type, to deliver pages and keep the site secure. If you email us, we use what you send only to reply.

Our apps

Each app has its own privacy notice describing what it collects, why, who it’s shared with, and how to delete it. We publish that notice on this site before the app is released and link to it from the app’s App Store and Google Play listings.

  • The Commons: notice published before release
  • Picture Daily: notice published before release
  • Fanthology: notice published before release

Your choices

You can ask what information we hold about you, or ask us to correct or delete it, by emailing daniel@thecommonsspeech.com.

Changes to this page

If we change this page, we’ll update the date at the top.
